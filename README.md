# Academic Projects

Coursework from my BSc in Analytics and Sustainability Studies at the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai. Most of it uses UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) data to compare countries and track progress, built with Power BI, Excel, MATLAB and Python.

![SDG Power BI dashboard: goal performance mapped for one country](01-sdg-powerbi-dashboard/images/country-goal-maps.jpg)

## Projects

| # | Project | What it is | Tools |
|---|---------|-----------|-------|
| 01 | [SDG Performance Dashboard](01-sdg-powerbi-dashboard) | Interactive dashboard tracking SDGs 3, 4, 6, 8 and 13 for every country, 2000–2022, with drill-through by country and indicator | Power BI, Power Query |
| 02 | [BRICS Sustainability Report](02-brics-sdg-report) | Ranks Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa on SDG indicators (2015–2022) and writes up the trends | Excel, UN SDG database |
| 03 | [SAARC SDG Indicators Dashboard](03-saarc-sdg-dashboard) | Filterable dashboard comparing South Asian countries on poverty, hunger, maternal health, emissions and electricity access | Excel pivot charts |
| 04 | [National Energy Profile Dashboards](04-energy-profile-dashboards) | Energy demand, mix, emissions and electrification from 1990 to 2020, including a Bangladesh profile | Excel |
| 05 | [Storm Events Analysis](05-storm-events-analysis-matlab) | Finds the US states hit hardest by hurricane damage in 2018 and maps the events | MATLAB, NOAA data |
| 06 | [Speech Signal Processing](06-speech-signal-processing) | Speech-to-text in English and Hindi, plus waveform, spectrogram and formant analysis | Python, librosa, Praat |
| 07 | [Uttarakhand Forest Fires 2016](07-uttarakhand-forest-fires-2016) | Disaster case study: causes, impacts, response and recommendations (group project) | Report |
| 08 | [SABLA Scheme Social Impact Assessment](08-sabla-social-impact-assessment) | Problem-tree and objective-tree assessment of a national scheme for adolescent girls | Report |
| 09 | [Optimization for Real-World Problems](09-optimization-real-world-problems) | Linear programming models for vaccine distribution, farming, household water use and carbon sequestration | Excel Solver |

Also included: two [infographics](infographics) made for TISS awareness campaigns.
