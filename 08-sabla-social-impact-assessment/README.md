# SABLA Scheme: Social Impact Assessment

A social impact assessment of the Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Empowerment of Adolescent Girls (RGSEAG), better known as SABLA. This is an Indian government program for girls aged 11–18 that combines nutrition, health check-ups, counselling, life-skills education and vocational training. Written for the course *Environmental and Social Impact Assessment*.

## Contents

- the scheme's goals, target group and services
- a **problem tree** mapping the root causes and effects of the issues SABLA addresses
- an **objective tree** turning those problems into intended outcomes and activities
- gaps in design and implementation, with conclusions
