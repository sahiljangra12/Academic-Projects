# Optimization Techniques for Real-World Problems

A presentation that sets up and solves four linear programming problems in sustainability and public health. Each one defines decision variables, an objective function and constraints, solves the model with Excel Solver, and interprets the Solver answer report.

| Problem | Decision | Objective |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Vaccine distribution** | Doses allocated to each region | Allocate a fixed supply of 1,000 doses across three regions without exceeding any region's demand |
| **Organic farming** | Acres allocated to each crop | Maximize total yield within land and fertilizer limits |
| **Household water use** | Daily water for drinking, cooking and laundry | Minimize total use while meeting minimum needs and a daily cap |
| **Carbon sequestration** | Acres for afforestation, soil carbon and mangrove restoration | Maximize carbon captured within land and budget limits |
